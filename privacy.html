Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Night Ecosystem (“Night”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our products including Night Protocol (NTP), NightGrid, NightCap, and NightAgent.

Effective date: [INSERT DATE] • Version: v0.1 • Domain: night.pi

Scope Data We Collect Public Blockchain & Permanent Storage How We Use Data Sharing Retention Security Your Rights Children International Transfers Changes Contact

1) Scope

This policy applies to your use of Night services and websites (collectively, the “Services”), including any apps or web interfaces that integrate authentication or payments (e.g., through the Pi ecosystem).

Third-party services (such as Pi Wallet, Pi SDK, Arweave gateways, analytics providers, or exchanges) may have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies as well.

2) Data We Collect

2.1 Account / Identity Data

  • Pi authentication identifiers (if you log in through Pi SDK), such as a user identifier token provided by Pi.
  • Wallet addresses (Pi address, and/or other blockchain addresses if used in the future).
  • Optional profile data you choose to provide (e.g., username, display name, bio), if supported.

2.2 Content You Create

  • Text content you submit to NightGrid (e.g., posts, messages, idea descriptions).
  • Metadata related to your content (timestamps, references, indexing IDs).

2.3 Payments & Transaction Data

  • Payment IDs and verification details received from payment providers (e.g., Pi SDK payment APIs).
  • Transaction hashes and related blockchain metadata (public by nature on most networks).
  • Internal credits/usage records (if we use a credit-based system for better UX).

2.4 Device & Usage Data

  • Basic device and browser information (e.g., device type, OS, language, approximate region, app version).
  • Log data for reliability and security (e.g., request timestamps, error logs, IP address).
  • Analytics events (if enabled) such as page views and feature usage.

2.5 NightAgent (Offline Features) Data

If you use NightAgent offline features (e.g., NFC/cards/access codes), we may process:

  • Offline transaction events and reconciliation records when you reconnect.
  • Security-related tokens (e.g., one-time codes, nonces) used to prevent replay and fraud.
Important: You can choose to hide your real-world identity. However, if you publish content publicly, your content may still be linked to an address or identifier, depending on your settings and the underlying platform.

3) Public Blockchain & Permanent Storage (Arweave)

Some Night features are designed to create durable records. When you choose to publish content permanently:

  • Your content (or a representation of it) may be stored on decentralized networks such as Arweave.
  • Once stored, content may be public and permanent and cannot be fully deleted by Night or anyone else.
  • We may store pointers such as an Arweave Transaction ID (e.g., arweave_tx_id) in our indexing database for usability.
Data permanence warning: If you post sensitive personal information, you may not be able to remove it later. Please publish responsibly.

4) How We Use Data

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the Services (login, payments, content publishing, retrieval, indexing).
  • Verify payments and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • Maintain system reliability (debugging, monitoring, performance tuning).
  • Improve features and user experience (product analytics, feedback, A/B testing where applicable).
  • Comply with legal obligations (where applicable).

5) Sharing of Information

We may share information in these cases:

5.1 Service Providers

We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, databases, monitoring) who process data on our behalf under contractual obligations.

5.2 Blockchain / Decentralized Networks

When you choose blockchain-based or permanent storage features, relevant data may be published to public networks. Such data is accessible by anyone and is outside our direct control.

5.3 Legal / Safety

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Services.

5.4 Business Transfers

If Night is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable laws and this policy.

6) Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

  • Indexing records (e.g., user ↔ content pointers) may be retained to enable retrieval.
  • Payment verification logs may be retained for fraud prevention and accounting.
  • Public blockchain / Arweave data may be permanent and not erasable.

7) Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. These may include:

  • Access controls and least-privilege policies
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and, where appropriate, encryption at rest
  • Secure key management (private keys never stored in front-end code)
  • Audit logs and anomaly detection for abuse prevention

No system is 100% secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your device, wallet, and login credentials.

8) Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights such as:

  • Access: request a copy of certain personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: request corrections to inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: request deletion of certain data (where possible)
  • Objection / Restriction: object to or restrict certain processing
  • Portability: request your data in a portable format (where applicable)
Limitations: If your content or transactions are recorded on public blockchain networks or Arweave, we may not be able to delete or alter that data. We can, where feasible, remove references from our indexing systems.

9) Children

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10) International Data Transfers

Night may operate globally. Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. We take steps to protect your data when transferred internationally, subject to applicable law.

11) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Effective date” and provide notice where appropriate.
